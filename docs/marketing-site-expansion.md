# Marketing site expansion plan

**Status:** Partially implemented; the salon page, 12 other first-wave industry pages, a hotel page, and the industry directory are implemented. The three-hub taxonomy below, additional audience pages, and product, tool, comparison, and editorial expansion remain planned work. Nothing is live in production; the agreed URL replacement needs no redirects.
**Plan updated:** 2026-10-16, including the user-supplied keyword lists, browser-only tool priority, and researched page-section briefs. The briefs specify planned content, not implemented pages.
**Research checked:** 2026-10-09. **Wix sitemap reference:** User-provided snapshot dated 2026-10-14; not independently crawled. Competitor page structures change; recheck them before implementation.

## Goal

Expand Velozy's marketing site to answer the specific questions people search before choosing a website builder, help visitors start a site, and attract relevant organic traffic. Pages should explain a real Velozy use case and lead naturally to creating a website. Do not promise rankings, traffic, or conversion outcomes.

## What exists today

- The marketing app serves a homepage, `/pricing`, `/industries`, and JSON-backed pages at `/industries/[slug]`. Each industry JSON supplies its route slug, palette, metadata, previews, sections, and FAQs; `apps/marketing/src/content/solutions/index.ts` registers and validates the entries. All 13 pages use the salon-derived section layout. Pages include supplied imagery where available; missing assets render black image slots. Utility and management previews are illustrative and describe unavailable specialised workflows as enquiries or external-provider paths. The current configuration has no legacy salon redirect; unknown legacy solution URLs have no page.
- Velozy generates responsive business websites from a business profile and page plan. Approved pricing allows one free homepage on a Velozy subdomain, with additional pages/custom domains on paid plans. Platform checkout, subscription webhooks and entitlement/credit code exist; live activation and staging verification remain governed by [platform billing setup](./platform-billing.md).
- The repository contains publishing, forms/enquiries, CMS, commerce and substantial SEO campaign/research/measurement implementations. Do not describe those entire systems as absent or infer production readiness from source presence. Follow the [SEO manual](./features/agentic-seo-manual.md) and its evidence log for SEO status. Native booking lifecycles and the onboarding AI-logo workflow remain planned; the [industry readiness matrix](./features/industry-readiness/README.md) records the inspected gaps.
- The [onboarding and generation plan](./features/onboarding.md) targets beginner-friendly discovery and all advertised industries with applicable core native workflows at launch. The [capability plans](./features/industry-readiness/README.md) own implementation details. Until their release gates pass, keep current enquiry/external-provider disclosures and do not advertise unavailable workflows as live.
- There is no public standalone tools directory in the marketing app today. Existing internal image editing or SEO APIs do not by themselves make a public free tool available.

Before publishing any offer page, confirm the matching capability, entitlement, current price, and working signup path. Keep marketing copy consistent with `pricing_config.json` and the product's actual release state.

### Competitive gap and Velozy's angle

The competitors reviewed cover three different offers: large browsable template inventories (Wix, Webflow, Squarespace), focused AI site generation (Kleap, Durable, Hostinger), and adjacent business utilities (Kleap, Squarespace, Durable). Velozy's most credible near-term organic opportunity is to connect its existing business-profile/page-plan generation to concrete small-business website needs. Its pages should show what the user supplies, the pages Velozy can generate, what the user can edit, what publishing/plan limits apply, and a real preview or example. A long tool catalog, unverified customer examples, unsupported integrations, or a promise to rank would weaken that offer.

Competitor page structures establish content patterns and query themes, not demand. The keyword examples below are intent targets, not volume estimates. A competitor's large sitemap is a taxonomy reference, not evidence that Velozy should publish the same number of pages. Before adding the next wave, check Velozy Search Console and localized SERP/keyword data; record source, country/language, date, intent, and existing Velozy URL to avoid overlap.

### Rules for every page brief

- Address the visitor's task first. Use one descriptive H1, logically ordered H2 sections and H3 subtopics where useful. An interactive control group need not become an extra marketing section.
- Use the primary query naturally in the title and H1. Use related terms only where they help explain the task; do not repeat exact-match phrases in every heading or impose keyword density, minimum word count or a fixed number of FAQs.
- Write concrete headings such as “Show salon services and prices”, not “Everything your salon site needs to say hello”. Explain inputs, outputs, steps, cost and limitations in plain language; avoid slogans, invented proof and generic “why choose us” filler.
- Give each page a unique meta description summarising its actual task, output and relevant limit. Keep metadata and visible content consistent. Do not promise rankings, traffic, rich results, perfect previews or universal compatibility.
- Answer a question once. Remove overlapping FAQs, including the current repeated nonprofit donation/volunteer questions. Keep educational guides separate from these commercial/tool pages.
- Include a small number of useful related links. Do not add every tool or audience link to every page. Use FAQ structured data only when appropriate to the visible content; do not promise FAQ search features.

## Keyword and SEO workflow

1. Before launch, start with support/onboarding questions and localized keyword/SERP research; use verified Velozy Search Console data once the site is live. For every candidate record the keyword cluster, canonical destination, existing/new status, dependency, publication gate and research source/date. Deferred routes stay in this plan, not as public placeholders.
2. Group queries by intent and map one primary intent to one canonical page. Keep informational guides separate from commercial builder and solution pages.
3. Estimate value using product fit, intent, current visibility, conversion path, effort, and verified demand. Treat third-party volume/difficulty as estimates with source/date.
4. Create or update a page only when Velozy can provide distinct, accurate value for the searcher. Merge overlapping targets instead of competing with our own page.
5. Review indexing, impressions, clicks, and conversions after publication. Improve or retire pages that do not serve visitors; don't use traffic projections as guarantees.

For SEO campaigns, follow the [agentic SEO implementation manual](./features/agentic-seo-manual.md): do not invent search volume/rankings; get user confirmation before competitor crawling; respect module access and provider budgets; and do not publish pages automatically outside the user's authorization.
